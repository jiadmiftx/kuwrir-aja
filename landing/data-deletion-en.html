Request Data Deletion

Delete specific personal data from Cocourir without deleting your account

Last updated: July 3, 2026 — this page complements Cocourir's Privacy Policy, Sections 6 & 7.

What is this?

You don't have to delete your entire Cocourir account just to remove specific data. This page explains which data can be deleted separately, and how to submit a request.

Data that can be deleted without deleting your account

DataDeletable separately?
Profile photo✓ Yes
Old verification documents (previous ID/driving licence/vehicle registration versions, once replaced)✓ Yes
Support chat history✓ Yes
Saved order notes/preferences (not transaction records)✓ Yes
Transaction & financial records (completed orders, payouts)✗ No, while account is active
Core account data (name, phone number, email)✗ No, while account is active

Transaction records and core account data must be retained while your account is active, to meet financial record-keeping obligations and to keep the service (orders, payouts, support) working. If you want this data removed too, request full account deletion via the same channel below.

How to submit a request

⏱ Processed within 7 business days
  1. Email halo@cocourir.com from the address registered to your account (or include your registered phone number). This lets us verify the request genuinely comes from the account owner.
  2. Use the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and specify which data you want deleted. See the sample email format below.
  3. Our team will verify your identity and process the request within 7 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email once the data has been deleted.

Sample email format:

To: halo@cocourir.com Subject: Data Deletion Request Name: [Your name] Registered phone number: [08xx-xxxx-xxxx] App: [Customer App / Merchant App / Driver App] I would like to request deletion of the following data from my account: - [ ] Profile photo - [ ] Old verification documents - [ ] Support chat history - [ ] Other: [specify] I would like to keep using my account as normal.

Need your entire account deleted?

Use the same email channel above, just change the subject to "Account Deletion Request". Note that certain transaction data may still be retained as required by law even after your account is deleted/deactivated — see Privacy Policy Section 7 for details.
