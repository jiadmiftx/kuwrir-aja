# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Admin and operations staff of KUWRIR food delivery platform. Working on desktop browsers to manage day-to-day operations: verifying new merchants/drivers, monitoring orders, processing COD settlements, handling promotions. Indonesian-speaking, local to Kuta, Lombok. Frequently checks dashboards mid-shift; speed and clarity matter more than visual novelty.

## Product Purpose

KUWRIR Admin is the backoffice for a COD-only food delivery platform serving Kuta, Lombok. It manages the full operator workflow: merchant onboarding and verification, driver applications, live order monitoring, cash settlement tracking, and promotion management. Success means an operator can complete their daily reconciliation and verification tasks without friction.

## Brand Personality

Local, warm, operational. KUWRIR is a community-scale food delivery platform — not a global SaaS tool. The interface should feel like a well-run local business: dependable, warm in personality, unapologetically food-forward. Three words: **lokal, tangguh, menggiurkan** (local, sturdy, appetizing).

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS admin dashboards (Vercel/Linear aesthetic: cold gray, monochrome, iceberg-minimal). This platform has warmth and food personality.
- Overly corporate fintech dashboards (navy + gold, hard edges, no personality).
- Cream/sand/warm-neutral backgrounds as a default AI aesthetic.

## Design Principles

1. **Food-forward identity** — the product handles food; the UI should carry that energy without being a restaurant menu.
2. **Operators in flow** — minimize friction. Dense when needed (tables, stats), breathing room for decision screens.
3. **Warm clarity** — use the food brand warmth in accents and key moments, not as a surface wash.
4. **Local confidence** — no need to mimic global SaaS tools. Own the local, community-scale identity.
5. **State honesty** — every action has visible feedback. Loading, error, empty, success states are first-class.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Operators may use this on varying screen brightness conditions (field checks, low-light offices). Reduced motion support required.
